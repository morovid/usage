Privacy policy.

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Usage is a macOS app by Morovid that helps you monitor your AI accounts. This policy covers the app and this website.

We do not collect your app data

Usage does not send your account details, credentials, balances, or usage readings to Morovid. We do not operate a backend that receives this information. The app has no analytics, advertising, tracking, or automatic crash reporting to us.

We do not sell, rent, or share your app data with advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties.

Your information stays on your Mac

Account settings and preferences are stored locally. Credentials managed by Usage are stored in macOS Keychain. Some sign-in integrations use the provider's official tools to manage their own credentials. Usage readings are kept in memory.

Local-session detection checks supported credential locations on your Mac. Importing a session requires your action; it does not upload your credentials to us.

Connections to your providers

When you connect an account, Usage communicates directly with that provider to authenticate and retrieve account usage or billing information. These requests include the credentials and identifiers needed for the connection. We do not receive a copy.

Your providers process these requests under their own privacy policies. Opening provider sign-in pages, GitHub, or other external links is also subject to those services' policies.

This website

We do not add analytics, advertising trackers, or tracking cookies to this website. Fonts and other page assets are hosted with the site.

Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, may process standard connection information, such as your IP address and request details, to deliver and secure the website. See Cloudflare's privacy policy. App downloads hosted on GitHub are subject to GitHub's privacy statement.

You are in control

You can remove connected accounts in Usage. This removes the credentials and account records managed for them by Usage, but does not necessarily revoke access at the provider or sign out other tools. You can revoke credentials directly with your provider.

Because we do not receive your app data, we have no server-side copy to access or delete.

Questions and updates

For privacy questions, contact Morovid. If you choose to contact us, we receive the information you include so we can respond. Please do not send API keys or access tokens.

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
